Three things we are known for

The sushi

Cut to order at the counter — nigiri, sashimi and rolls, built from the day's fish rather than a fixed list. The thing people come back for.

02

The oven

Beyond the raw menu, dishes come out of the Japanese oven — smoked eel among them. The half of the kitchen most guests don't expect.

03

The terrace

Tables facing open water on the Old Town beachfront. Service from half past two until half past eleven, every day.

Selected dishes

From the kitchen

A few of the plates guests write home about. The full card is at the table.

Nigiri & sashimi

Cut to order. Salmon, tuna, and whatever came in that morning — presented plainly, because it doesn't need help.

Smoked eel

Out of the Japanese oven. Rich, sweet, and the dish our regulars order before they've opened the menu.

Seafood in coconut

Mixed fish and seafood in a light coconut sauce. The Asian half of Asian fusion, and the plate we'd point a first-timer at.

Tom kha kai

Coconut, galangal, chicken. Ordered as a starter, remembered as a main event.

Mövenpick ice cream

We don't make it and we won't pretend we do. It's very good, and it ends the meal properly.
